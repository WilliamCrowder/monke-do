Quest data
----------
Data/MonkeDB.lua is derived in part from the Questie database:
  https://github.com/Questie/Questie
  https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB
published under the GNU GPL v3 by the Questie project and its contributors.
Monke Do takes quest titles, objective sentences, target names and turn-in names from it,
and adds its own summaries and task lines. The generated file states the build and date it
was made. The pipeline that makes it is in the Monke Do repository.

World of Warcraft and its quest text are the property of Blizzard Entertainment.
