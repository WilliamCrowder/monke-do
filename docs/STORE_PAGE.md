Paste-ready text for the CurseForge and Wago project pages. Both take Markdown.

## Name

Monke Do

## Summary (the one-liner under the name)

Replaces long quest text with a one-line story and a simple task list, points you to your next
objective, and switches back to Blizzard's text in one click.

Shorter, if a field limits it: "Short quest summaries and simple task lists for WoW: Forever, with
a pointer to your next objective."

## Description

**Quest text for smooth brains.**

Tired of scrolling through paragraphs of quest text just to find out you need ten bandanas?
Monke Do boils every quest down to the story in one sentence and the steps in a short list, with
your progress right on it. Want the whole story? One click on the Wrinkle button brings back
Blizzard's original text, word for word.

> Bad men wear red hankies. Scout lady want them.
> • Grab 10 Red Silk Bandana (3/10)
> • Go back to Scout Riell.
> Monke get: 875 XP, 4s

Built for players who skim, players with ADHD, and anyone who would rather play than read.

### What you get

- **Short quest text everywhere.** Quest window, quest log and map, with live progress on every
  task.
- **The Now line.** The one thing to do next and how far away it is, right under your character.
  A monke finger points the way and turns as you turn. It fades during fights and glows when you
  are back.
- **Monke pick.** Can't decide? One key picks for you: quests you can hand in first, then the
  nearest one. Press again for the next.
- **The Monke Do list.** Every quest's next step, nearest first, re-sorted as you walk. Click a
  line to follow it.
- **Two voices.** Monke ("Bad men wear red hankies.") or Plain English, the same information
  without the joke.
- **"OK, monke do!"** Voice lines when you take a quest. Mute them any time.
- **Over 5,000 quests, including Forever's new ones.** A quest the database hasn't seen yet still
  gets a task list, never a wall of text.

### Plays nice with other addons

Monke Do changes the words, not the map. It adds no map pins, so it runs happily alongside
Questie or any other quest helper: they show where, Monke Do says what.

### Your data stays yours

No tracking, no network, nothing collected. Monke Do saves only your own settings.

### Commands

`/monke` settings · `/monke lore` switch texts · `/monke pick` pick a quest (or bind a key) ·
`/monke list on` the list · `/monke voice plain` plain English · `/monke help` everything else

### Open source

Free and open source under the GPL v3. Quest titles and objectives come from the Questie
project's database (QuestieDB, GPL v3); questions about that data belong with QuestieDB. Missing
or wrong quest? Report the quest ID on GitHub and it will be in the next update.
